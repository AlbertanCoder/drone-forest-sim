# Find the fire: drone training simulation

A training simulation in your browser: fly a small drone with a thermal camera over a made-up forest, find a fire
(from a smouldering spot to a big plume) and report it. Then land and stay landed while the helicopter works.

Play it on a computer (keyboard or a game controller) or on a phone or tablet (two thumb sticks). Tap Play to fly,
or Watch a demo first. On an iPhone, for full screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Settings, Realism: Training (the default), Real or Easy, or your own mix.

Made-up forest; training simulation. It is not flight advice: in real life a drone of 250 g or more needs a pilot
certificate and registration, must stay in sight, and must keep away from wildfires and firefighting aircraft.
