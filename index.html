    Training simulation · made-up forest, northern Alberta
    
    
    Find the fire
    
    
    Easy radio is on. Real radios lose signal through trees: turn it off in Settings for the real challenge.
    
    
    Fly the drone over a forest where a fire has started. Its thermal camera finds hot spots with the same code as the real payload. Your job is only to find the fire and press Report hot spot. Responders fly in and drop water where you reported.
    
    
    
    
    How to play
    
    Each game the fire starts somewhere new in a 10 km forest, at a random stage, from a smouldering spot to a big plume. A lookout gives you a rough direction. You have two battery packs: swap them by hand at the take-off spot.
    
    
      
      
    1. Arm and take off.
      2. 
      
    2. Fly over the forest and watch the thermal picture (top right). The fire shows as a white hot spot with a circle round it.
      3. 
      
    3. Over the fire, turn the nose to north, hover level, then press Report hot spot. The real payload maps hot spots as if the drone faced north and looked straight down, so a report made facing another way, or in fast flight, lands in the wrong place.
      4. 
      
    4. A helicopter drops water where you reported. A careless report misses.
      5. 
    
    
    
    Controls
      
    Keyboard: Space arm / disarm · W / S climb / sink (or the throttle, with height hold off) · arrow keys or I J K L tilt · A / D or Q / E turn · R report · C camera · G height hold on / off · M angle / acro · H or ? the controls panel
    
      
    Gamepad: left stick up / down climb / sink and sideways turn, right stick tilt, Start arms.
    
      
    Phone: the left thumb stick is up / down (it stays where you leave it; the middle holds the height) and turn; the right one tilts. The Arm button arms.
    
      
    Your RadioMaster Pocket: plug it in by USB, choose USB Joystick (HID) on the radio, then press Set up radio.

      Flying help
      
      
      
      
    The middle of the throttle holds the height. The real drone has no height hold: switch it off to fly it for real.
    
      
      
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
      Sound
      
      
      
      
    The motors, the wind, the fire and the radio's warnings. Nothing plays until you press Play.
    
    
    
    
    
      Weather and time of day
      
    
      
      
      
      
    Mid-October, at this forest's latitude (56° N, a made-up spot in northern Alberta). The sun, the light, the haze and the wind follow these. At night the flying camera sees little; the thermal camera still works.
